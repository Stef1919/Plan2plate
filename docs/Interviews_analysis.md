# POST INTERVIEW ANALYSIS

**Denis**, 29, work-from-home developer, cooks spontaneously, shops infrequently, and values quality and convenience over discounts or planning.

**Simon**, 34, busy bank worker, values convenience and flexibility, relies on discounts and store brands, and struggles more with cooking motivation than meal planning.

**Liam**, 21, chemistry student, is a structured meal planner who values healthy variety, uses shopping lists, and balances price and quality.

**Sven**, 26, factory worker, prefers simple, spontaneous cooking, dislikes meal planning, and values quality while occasionally considering discounts.

## KEY TAKEAWAYS:
- Support both planners and spontaneous users.
- Provide simple recipe suggestions and quick meals.
- Recipes based on discounted ingredients.
- Automatic shopping lists generated from recipes.
- Prioritize quality and convenience.
