## POSITIVE AND NEGATIVE TEAM PATTERNS
## NEGATIVE PATTERNS:
## 1. Ignore Human Issues
For this university project there were many times we became stressed and overwhelmed
about deadlines and requirements without openly talking about it.
## 2. Be Everyone’s Friend
There were many times when honest feedback was needed but not given due to keeping the
tensions low with classmates, since we don’t know each other that well.
## 3. Ignore Low Performers
On some deadlines and one ore more team members contributed less work, while the rest
of the group avoided confronting the issue.

## POSITIVE PATTERNS:
## 4. Set Clear Goals
We made sure to set clear goals for the project since we were constantly working under time
pressure and were balancing multiple subjects.
- Be a Teacher and a Mentor
Our team members have very different strengths, so sharing knowledge improved
collaboration and learning.

## HOW WE ADRESS THE PATTERNS IN OUR PROJECT:
- We scheduled short weekly check-ins where we could discuss our project progress and
issues and redistribute the tasks if one member becomes overloaded.
- We clearly defined responsibilities from the beginning and use shared tools for
communication.
- We divided tasks clearly, but we should have tracked progress more regularly and talked
directly with members who fell behind.
- We broke the project into smaller tasks and mostly followed the deadlines through.
- We shared the resources and tutorials needed to complete tasks.
