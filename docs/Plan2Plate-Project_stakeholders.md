**PROJECT STAKEHOLDERS**

**1\. MERCATOR (Investor & Retail partner)**

**Status:** Positive

**Expectations & interests:**

- Increase online grocery sales
- Gain insight into customer purchasing behaviour
- Improve customer loyalty

**Conflicts:** Pricing synchronization with physical store and accurate inventory data

**Benefits:** More frequent orders and increased basket size

**Team response:** Design a seamless integration with Mercator’s product database

**2\. USERS**

**Status:** Positive

**Expectations & interests:**

- Simplify meal planning and grocery shopping
- Save time deciding what to cook
- Access affordable and healthy meals

**Conflicts:** Resistance to changing grocery shopping habits

**Benefits:** Reduce stress, less food waste

**Team response:** Design must be intuitive and easy to use, trust must be built through transparency.

**3\. MERCATOR CUSTOMERS**

**Status:** Neutral to Positive

**Expectations & interests:**

- Good prices and discounts
- Fast and simple grocery shopping

**Conflicts:** May prefer to stick to traditional shopping

**Benefits:** Discover new way of shopping

**Team response:** Onboarding needs to be simple, platform must show clear value over in store shopping

**4\. COMPETITORS (online grocery stores, meal apps)**

**Status:** Negative

**Expectations & interests:**

- Competition for convenience and user retention

**Conflicts:** Compete in meal planning and healthy recipes

**Benefits:** Validation of market demand for our solution

**Team response:** Differentiate from the competitors through Mercator integration and option to meal plan and grocery shop in the same place
