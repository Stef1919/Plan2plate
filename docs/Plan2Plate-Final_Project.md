## 1. BUSINESS DESCRIPTION
First off, let’s make sure we’ve defined what we think this business is about. We want to bring
an easier way to grocery shop and plan your meals. To achieve our goal, we are collaborating
with Mercator and opening a new grocery shopping platform “Plate2Plan”. Our webstore will
allow users to purchase groceries through set meals they can add to their meal plan.

## 2. POSITIONING STATEMENT
Plan2Plate is made for busy professionals, students and health-conscious planners who struggle
with deciding what to cook and want a more efficient way to plan meals and shop groceries. It
is a digital meal-planning and grocery integration that simplifies weekly food decisions and
automatically connects recipes to real-time grocery shopping through Mercator.

## User Stories
Jakob the Math Teacher
Story 1: My work keeps me extremely busy – I can work between 8 an 14h a day. I want to
generate a weekly meal plan in under 15min so that I can save time and reduce decision
fatigue.

- System generates 7 days of meals – breakfast, lunch and dinner.
- User can replace any single meal without regenerating the whole week.
- User then saves the weekly plan.

often forget to buy some key ingredients for my meals. I’d like a shopping list so I don’t forget
ingredients.

- Duplicate ingredients are merged.
- User decides he doesn’t need a specific ingredient and removes it.

Story 3: On some weeks I don’t want to spend additional time grocery shopping after work. I
want to order all ingredients directly from Mercator so I don’t need to visit the store.
- After shopping list creation, user clicks “Add all to cart” button.
- System automatically adds all of the ingredients and re-routes the user to the cart.
- User can choose delivery or pickup.
- User can pre-pay or pay upon delivery/pickup.
- User receives order confirmation.

## Hypothesis Testing
## 1. CONDUCTING THE INTERVIEW AND HYPOTHESIS
We gathered 4 different participants that fit our personas and experience problems similar to
our problem scenarios. Each of them was presented with the same set of interview questions.
## Hypotheses:
- Users struggle with deciding what to cook.
- Users want faster and simpler meal planning/shopping.
- Automatically generated shopping lists would reduce forgotten ingredients and extra
store visits.
- Busy users value convenience features such as online ordering, delivery, and pickup.
- Discounts and sales can strongly influence grocery purchases and recipe decisions.
- Users prefer personalized meal suggestions instead of browsing large amounts of
recipes.
- Health-conscious users want nutritional information and balanced meal planning.
- Users want to reuse old meal plans and recipes frequently.
- Flexible meal planning durations better fit different lifestyles and planning habits.
- Users value quality products, but many still balance quality with affordability.

platform Plan2Plate in collaboration with Mercator. Our goal is to determine whether users
can:
▪ easily decide what to cook

▪ order groceries
The test will be conducted on at least three participants, each representative of a key
persona. Participants will be asked to complete realistic tasks while using the thinking aloud
method.
